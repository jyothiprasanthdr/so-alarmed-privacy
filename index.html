This Privacy Policy explains how So Alarmed ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects your information. So Alarmed is an anti-snooze alarm clock app for iPhone that helps you build reliable morning habits by completing proof-of-wake missions.

The App is developed and operated by Nandha Kumar Ilangovan ("the Developer"), who is the data controller responsible for your information under this Policy.

We built So Alarmed with privacy as a foundational design principle. The App displays no banner or pop-up third-party ads, does not sell your personal data, does not access your contacts, camera, or microphone, and stores almost all your operational data directly on your device. We use privacy-preserving measurement tools (including Apple Search Ads via Apple's AdServices, RevenueCat, and aggregate SKAdNetwork) strictly to evaluate campaign performance and subscription delivery, with zero IDFA collection and zero cross-app tracking. By downloading or using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1. The short version

  • Fully functional offline: Alarms, wake routines, streak shields, and themes work on-device without requiring an account.
  • Optional cloud sync: You only get an account if you voluntarily choose to sign in (with Apple or Google) to back up your streak and alarms across devices.
  • Secure payments: Subscriptions and trials are processed directly through Apple StoreKit and verified via RevenueCat. We never see or store your payment card details.
  • On-device motion & sensors: Motion missions (e.g., Shake, Walk) and Bedside Nightstand dimming are computed locally in real time and discarded immediately. No sensor or camera data is ever stored or transmitted.
  • No advertising tracking (No IDFA / No ATT): We never collect Apple's Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA). We use Apple's privacy-preserving AdServices and aggregate SKAdNetwork for ad measurement.
  • Bedside Nightstand: Operates entirely on-device with zero-blue-light true OLED black pixels. No audio or ambient sound is recorded or transmitted.
  • Full account deletion: You can permanently delete your account and cloud records anytime in the App under Settings > Delete Account.

2. Information we collect and process

We collect only the minimum data necessary to provide reliable alarm functionality, subscription entitlements, and habit continuity.

2.1 Information stored locally on your device

Unless you explicitly sign in, the following remains entirely on your iPhone:

  • Alarms and Schedules: Time, custom label, alarm tone, wake-up mission (Math, Stroop, Memory, Barcode, Shake, Walk, Maze), difficulty level, repeat days, and enabled/disabled state.
  • Wake-up Streak & Streak Shields: Calendar dates of successfully dismissed alarms, current consecutive day streak, and active monthly streak shield quotas.
  • App Preferences: Onboarding completion state, active visual theme (e.g., Stealth OLED, Solar Dawn, Cyberpunk), and nightstand horology display preferences.

2.2 Account information (only if you choose to sign in)

Signing in is optional and exists only to back up your streak and sync alarms across devices. If you sign in using Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google, Google Firebase Authentication processes:

  • A unique pseudonymous user identifier (Firebase UID).
  • Your name, if provided by your sign-in method.
  • Your email address. When using Sign in with Apple, you may select Apple's Private Relay to keep your real email hidden.
  • Identity provider metadata (Apple or Google).

2.3 Cloud backup (only while signed in)

When you are signed in, the App backs up your alarm configurations, streak dates, shield balance, and profile state to your private record in Google Cloud Firestore. If you sign out or never sign in, no alarm or streak data is uploaded to our servers.

2.4 Subscription and Purchase Management (StoreKit & RevenueCat)

So Alarmed offers auto-renewing subscriptions, free trials, and promotional introductory pricing (such as downsell offers). Transactions are processed directly by Apple through In-App Purchase.

We partner with RevenueCat Inc. to manage subscription receipts, trial lifecycles, and entitlement validation. RevenueCat processes anonymous app user IDs, transaction identifiers, and receipt timestamps to ensure your Pro features remain unlocked across your devices. RevenueCat never receives your credit card or billing details. You can review RevenueCat's data practices in the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

2.5 Sensor Data (Processed live, never stored or transmitted)

The Shake and Walk missions access device accelerometer and pedometer data in real time solely to verify physical wakefulness. This sensor data is evaluated instantaneously on your device and immediately discarded. It is never logged, saved, or uploaded anywhere.

2.6 Bedside Nightstand Mode & Sleep Sounds

Nightstand Mode provides a bedside clock and ambient sleep audio (rain, ocean, white noise, etc.). All audio synthesis and playback occur locally on-device. The swipe-to-dim gesture controls screen brightness directly via iOS display APIs. The App never records audio, accesses the microphone, or monitors ambient sounds.

2.7 Local Notifications & AlarmKit

Alarms are scheduled on-device using Apple's AlarmKit framework (iOS 26+) or local notifications. These alerts are scheduled and managed by the operating system on your phone. We do not operate a push notification server to deliver wake alarms, and your alarms are never routed through external notification relays.

2.8 Campaign Attribution and Ad Measurement

To evaluate whether our App Store marketing campaigns are cost-effective, the App uses two privacy-preserving frameworks:

  • Apple Search Ads (AdServices.framework): When installed from an Apple Search Ad, the App requests a cryptographically signed attribution token from Apple's AdServices.framework. This token is sent to RevenueCat to attribute installs to keyword campaigns on an aggregate, non-personal basis. It does not access the IDFA and does not track you across apps.
  • Meta SDK (SKAdNetwork): Reports aggregate app events (e.g., install, trial start, purchase) to Meta Platforms using Apple's SKAdNetwork. FacebookAdvertiserIDCollectionEnabled is set to false in the App configuration; the IDFA is never collected or requested.

2.9 Product Telemetry and A/B Testing (Firebase Analytics)

We use Google Firebase Analytics to measure aggregate product health (e.g., onboarding funnel drop-offs, paywall conversion rates, and A/B cohort distribution). Events are tied to an anonymous Firebase App Instance ID and do not contain personal names, email addresses, or alarm labels.

2.10 What we NEVER collect

  • No precise or coarse GPS location.
  • No access to contacts, photo library, calendar events, camera, or microphone.
  • No health, biometrics, or medical records.
  • No advertising identifiers (IDFA) or third-party behavioral tracking profiles.
  • No sale or rental of personal information under any circumstances.

3. How we use your information

We use the limited data described above strictly to:

  • Schedule, ring, and resolve your alarms and wake-up missions.
  • Track and celebrate your wake-up streaks and manage streak shields.
  • Back up and restore your settings across devices when you choose to sign in.
  • Verify subscription status and unlock Pro themes, sounds, and unlimited missions.
  • Optimize onboarding flows and measure aggregate advertising efficiency.
  • Respond to direct support and privacy inquiries.

5. Data storage, security, and retention

Storage: Local settings, alarms, and streak records reside in your iPhone's encrypted local storage sandbox. Backed-up data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore servers with enterprise-grade encryption in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256).

Retention: Local data remains on your device until you delete alarms or uninstall the App. Cloud backup data is retained for the duration of your active account. When you delete your account (Settings > Delete Account), your record is permanently purged from Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore.

6. Your rights and data controls

  • Use without an account: You are never forced to create an account or sign in to use the App.
  • Delete your account: In the App, navigate to Settings > Delete Account. This permanently deletes your cloud profile, alarms, and streak data.
  • Revoke permissions: You can manage Motion & Fitness, Notifications, and Screen Time permissions anytime in the iOS Settings app.
  • Manage subscriptions: Cancel or change your subscription anytime in the App Store under Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions.
